Privacy policy – nBlock

Updated: 2026-10-12

Developer and contact

nBlock is provided by Q. An Ngu. Privacy inquiries: quangu95@gmail.com.

Purpose and local processing

nBlock links daily physical activity to time credit for apps you choose. The app has no user accounts, ads, analytics SDKs or backend. It requests no Internet permission and sends no data automatically to the developer.

Steps and Health Connect

With your permission, nBlock reads Health Connect step totals for daily progress, earned time and streaks. Supported devices also request background access so the active protection monitor and visible live wallpaper can update steps while the app is not on screen. Derived daily values and goals are stored locally. nBlock does not write Health Connect records. Resetting nBlock does not delete the original Health Connect data.

App selection and usage access

nBlock resolves names, icons and package names of launchable apps locally for protected-app selection. Android usage access lets nBlock detect foreground transitions and times when refreshing enabled protection and while the protection monitor runs. Usage durations for selected apps contribute to time credit and local history. Observation pauses when the screen is off or locked. Other app contents are not read.

Accessibility service

After your explicit consent and activation in Android, nBlock uses the AccessibilityService API for window changes and the visible app package name. Events are processed only while the protection monitor runs and the screen is unlocked. This improves protection after the Android app overview. nBlock does not read window contents, messages or typed text and performs no gestures. Package names and times may appear in local protection diagnostics.

Protection and live wallpaper

With your permission, nBlock shows its own protection screen over selected apps and local protection notifications. You can turn protection off in nBlock. The optional live wallpaper displays steps, time credit, protection status and, if selected, streaks. These values may be visible to anyone looking at your home or lock screen. You control activation and appearance in Android and nBlock.

Storage and retention

Data is held in private app storage on your device. Detailed history covers seven local calendar days and is pruned on reads and writes. Daily streak values and step goals have no automatic time-based expiry. Settings, selected apps, balance and usage checkpoints, and emergency-exit state remain until replaced or a full reset. Diagnostics retain at most 500 events, overwriting older entries. Versioned accessibility consent is stored locally.

Diagnostics export and sharing

Local diagnostics may include app package names, event times, steps, balance, protection status and technical errors. They contain no contents from other apps. Only after you confirm does nBlock pass the text to an app you choose. Review the log before sending. That app may process or transmit it under its own privacy practices. Resetting nBlock does not delete exported copies. nBlock does not sell health data or transfer it to advertising platforms.

Control, deletion and security

You can revoke Health Connect, usage, accessibility and overlay access in Android. Protection and live wallpaper can be disabled. Settings → Reset all data deletes nBlock data, including streaks, diagnostics and consent; Android closes the app. Uninstalling removes private app storage. App rules exclude Android backup and device transfer; device-specific behavior must be verified before publication. There are no app accounts.

Privacy inquiries

Contact the developer above about data handling and applicable privacy rights. The developer has no remote access through the app to your local data. Changes in data access will be described in this policy and, where needed, updated permission and consent flows.

Public privacy website

This is separate from local nBlock processing: app, health and usage data are not sent to the privacy website. The website uses no added analytics, external fonts or tracking scripts.
